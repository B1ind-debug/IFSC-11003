Who is FromSoftware?

FromSoftware is a Japanese video game developer known for creating challenging action role-playing games.

History and Prominent Members

Founded in 1986, FromSoftware has developed a reputation for its unique game design and storytelling. Key figures include Hidetaka Miyazaki, the director behind many of their acclaimed titles such as the Dark Souls series and Elden Ring.

Publishers

FromSoftware has been published by various companies throughout its history, including NIS America, Bandai Namco Entertainment, Sony, Sega and Capcom. These collaborations have brought some of their most popular games!

A Brief Portfolio of FromSoftware IPs

FromSoftware has developed a variety of games, but some of their most notable titles include:

  • King's Field series
  • Armored Core series
  • Souls series (Demon's Souls, Dark Souls, Bloodborne)
  • Elden Ring
  • Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice
